# Baseline and revision defect inventory

Baseline: deployed Daybreak source commit `4c7fee9` (public version 3), recovered before edits on 2026-09-05. The former full export and social cut are preserved separately. `baseline.jpg` is a decoded frame from the previous 1080p social export, not a browser screenshot.

## Directly observed or measured

- Finger pads can meet a target while the visible hand remains claw-like. The original hand placement used only a short forward offset relative to the MCP roots; that shortened the target distance and forced excessive bends.
- The former thumb patch moved only one intermediate joint. Current deep replay found actual thumb IP bending up to 103 degrees and incompatible distal twist despite an apparently improved outline.
- The first revised score/rig replay exposed wrist speeds above 9 m/s at 60 Hz. A 24 ms release gap left only 20 ms of wrist interpolation at some substitutions. A separate 120 Hz replay confirmed the problem.
- Some black-key octave poses reach the wrist-depth bound and miss their targets by about 10 mm. A lower depth bound and actual-pose fingering search are under review.
- The former clothing is a close-fitting tee/trouser surface without convincing seated folds or tailored edges. Facial curves and neckline/sleeve silhouette have visibly low mesh resolution at close range.
- The original surroundings are an abstract stage. The revision replaces this with a grounded recital room with coherent floor, walls, windows, ceiling, garden and daylight.
- The original piano strings are one coplanar fan; bridge shape and pin grid are unrelated to actual string endpoints. The former damper collection is empty. A shared course layout now connects these components.
- The original shoes are scaled spheres on rectangular soles. They are being replaced by shaped uppers and curved welt soles. Pedal height is reduced to support a less elevated right forefoot.
- Camera paths require renewed review because the room, body motion and music timing have changed.
